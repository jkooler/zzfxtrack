      Welcome to
      STRUDEL TO ZZFXM

      With this app you can create songs using ZzFXMicro.js synthesis and Strudel.

      After creating your song, export it to JSON format and use it in your games or other projects that use ZzFXM.

      NOTE: Ensure your project uses ZzFXMicro (standard for ZzFXM).
      The parameter mapping differs from the full ZzFX library.
