# Code of Conduct

## Expectations

Please keep participation in this project respectful and constructive.

This includes:

- assuming good faith
- focusing criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people
- avoiding harassment, abuse, discrimination, or personal attacks
- keeping technical disagreement direct but professional

## Scope

This applies to project discussions, issues, pull requests, and other public interactions in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or moderate comments, issues, or contributions that do not meet these expectations.

Repeated or severe violations may result in restricted participation.
